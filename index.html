Important engineering distinction: FiberIQ is a software-based engineering prototype. It does not directly measure a physical fibre using a VFL, OTDR or optical power meter.

A conventional Visual Fault Locator provides visible red-light indication of faults such as breaks, bends or leakage at accessible points. FiberIQ demonstrates how a software diagnostic layer can use available diagnostic measurements and known network topology to assist fault diagnosis and localization.

The simulated ground-truth fault is retained only to evaluate the accuracy of the localization algorithm. It is deliberately separated from the diagnostic calculation.
